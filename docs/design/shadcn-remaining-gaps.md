# Remaining non-official UI after #254

Audit of `origin/main` at 238e63d (after PR #274), 2026-10-24. Every production `.tsx`
outside `src/components/ui/` and `src/components/ai-elements/` was read in full
(151 files; tests and `prototypes/` excluded), plus both stylesheets and the AI
Elements catalogue on ui.shadcn.com.

The #263 inventory marks 229 rows `migrated`. That inventory counted a row as migrated
when an official component was imported. This audit asks a stricter question: does the
official component own the structure **and** the appearance? By that measure there are
**320 open findings in 111 files**, plus 77 places reviewed and accepted as-is.

| Kind | Count | Meaning |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| handcrafted | 98 | Built from raw HTML or utility classes although an official component exists |
| partial | 99 | Official component present, but a sub-part is rebuilt by hand or the wrong primitive is used |
| override | 84 | Official component used, but the project repaints its size, padding, border, font or colour |
| unused-official | 39 | The matching component is already installed and not used |
| exception-ok | 77 | Reviewed, justified (layout-only class, specialist engine, no official equivalent) |

## Status

UI2 slice A (#277, [ADR 0255](../adr/0255-official-components-own-their-variants-and-the-parallel-layer-is-retired.md))
closed patterns 1 and 11 and part of 7 and 12: the rows marked
`fixed (A #277)` below are done, the rows marked `exception-ok (A #277)` are
pass-throughs over official parts with no CSS. The remaining rows keep their
kind; `scripts/css-ownership-baseline.json` lists the CSS side of them by
owner.

## The twelve patterns behind most findings

1. **A second component system in `src/components/workspace/`.** `ViewHeader`,
   `DecisionButton`, `IconButton`, `StatusBadge`, `MemberIdentity`, `SendButton`/
   `StopButton`/`PauseButton` and `ActionTooltip` wrap official parts, then own typography,
   sizing and colour through `kubecode.css` (`.kubecode-view-header-*`,
   `.kubecode-decision-button`, `.kubecode-member-identity-*`). Nearly every view
   imports these instead of `Badge`, `Button`, `Item`, `Card`, `Tooltip`, `Toggle`,
   `PromptInputSubmit` directly. `ConversationIdentity` and `PromptDecision` duplicate
   two of them a second time.
2. **The Session (coding) view is the least official surface.** Scroller and jump button
   are hand-rolled (`AiPanelChrome.tsx:282-346`) although `Conversation`/
   `ConversationScrollButton` are installed; the composer is a contentEditable editor
   (`InlineWikilinkInput`/`InlineWikilinkParts`, ~700 lines, three bespoke listboxes,
   hand-made chips and placeholder) inside an `InputGroup`, not `PromptInput`/
   `PromptInputTextarea`/`PromptInputCommand`; `PromptInputSubmit` has its status/stop
   machinery bypassed; `ComposerAddMenu`, `ComposerCapabilityPicker` and
   `ComposerProjectWorkPicker` are Popover+Command hybrids with hand-drawn borders and
   raw `<p>` states.
3. **Two markdown stacks** (closed by #278, ADR 0255). `MarkdownContent.tsx` (react-markdown + rehype-highlight +
   hljs theme) shipped and was used by `DmShareDialog`; `index.css` `.ai-markdown`
   (lines 625-791) wrapped even the official `MessageResponse` output in `AiMessage.tsx:370`,
   so the legacy stylesheet overrides streamdown typography in the Session view.
4. **Appearance CSS attached to official components by className.** About 110 lines of
   `kubecode.css` repaint official parts: `SelectTrigger.kubecode-channel-select`,
   `DialogContent.kubecode-path-picker-dialog`, `Button.kubecode-file-tree-row`,
   `Button.kubecode-terminal-navigator-row`, `Message`/`MessageContent`/`MessageResponse`/
   `MessageAction` bubble rules (1822-1855), `PromptInputTextarea.kubecode-composer-textarea`,
   `ConversationScrollButton.kubecode-scroll-latest`, `Avatar.kubecode-conversation-avatar`,
   `HoverCardContent.kubecode-identity-card`. The ownership checker cannot see any of these
   because it only matches `[data-slot]`, `[data-radix]`, `[cmdk-]` and `!important`.
5. **Work Kanban and Context workbench are still drawn by CSS.** `kubecode-work-*`
   (columns, closed lane, card title/description/footer/counters, inspector frame and facts)
   and `kubecode-context-workbench`/`kubecode-explorer-section`/`kubecode-git-*`
   (panel frame, section headers, git rows, editor header, two dialogs) own borders,
   backgrounds, radius and 11-13px typography around a `Card` whose padding is zeroed.
6. **Shell chrome is hand-drawn.** `.kubecode-topbar`, `.kubecode-detail-pane`/
   `.kubecode-inspector`, `.kubecode-destination-header`, `.kubecode-channel-thread`,
   `.kubecode-work-inspector`, `.kubecode-session-header` paint background and hairlines
   instead of `Sidebar`/`ResizablePanel`/`Sheet` + `Separator` with semantic tokens.
7. **Installed components bypassed.** `CommandDialog` (rebuilt by `GlobalCommandPalette`),
   `CommandEmpty`/`CommandLoading` (raw `<p>` states in 6 pickers), `ScrollArea` (5 hand-made
   overflow boxes), `SidebarMenuBadge`/`SidebarMenuSkeleton`, `Toggle` (Buttons with
   `aria-pressed`), `ToggleGroup`, `InputGroup`, `AlertDialog` (inline two-step confirm in
   `MemberRoster`, Dialog in `TeamWorkspaceView`), `Collapsible`/`Accordion` (hand toggles
   in `MemberPanel`, `ResearchImportPanel`, `WorkInspector`, `TaskDisclosure`), `Kbd`,
   `Skeleton`, `Sonner` (not installed; two hand-made toast hosts).
8. **AI Elements used as a shell, not as the element.** `Plan` without `PlanHeader`,
   `Task` without `TaskTrigger`, `Sources` as a bare wrapper around `Item p-0`,
   `Confirmation` with project `DecisionButton`s and never `ConfirmationAccepted`/
   `ConfirmationRejected`, `Queue`'s `QueueItem` stripped and an `Item` nested inside,
   `Context` forced into click-popover semantics with its usage rows rewritten,
   `Reasoning`/`Task`/`Subagent` triggers replaced with hand-placed children,
   `Tool` never given `errorText`, `CodeBlock` without header/copy parts while copy is
   re-implemented three times.
9. **Agent-facing decisions outside `Confirmation`.** Team lineup proposal, user-input
   requests, suggested brief changes, unknown-state reconcile, task questions and
   side-question answers are built from `Card`/`Item`/`Alert` instead of `Confirmation`,
   `Suggestion`, `Message`/`MessageResponse` or `PromptInput`.
10. **Raw lists.** `<ul>/<li>` and `<dl>` rows in ~20 places (MemberRoster, TaskBoardView,
    TaskActivity, TaskOverview, ActivityGroup, OutboxRows, ExecutionStrip, MessageItem,
    research sources/artifacts/decisions, WorkInspector facts) where `ItemGroup`, `Queue`,
    `Sources`/`Source` or `Table` exist.
11. **Per-call size overrides.** `pointer-coarse:min-h-11` / `min-h-(--ws-touch-target)` /
    `.kubecode-touch-target` on ~30 Buttons and SidebarMenuButtons, `Empty className="p-4"`
    at 14 sites, `FieldLabel className="font-normal"` at 8, `Card`/`Item`/`Dialog`/`Sheet`
    padding zeroed, `Button variant=link h-auto p-0` — all of which belong in a size variant
    of the component, once.
12. **Catalogue drift.** The shadcn-hosted AI Elements catalogue now has `File Tree`
    (→ `ProjectFileTree`, git change list), `Model Selector` (→ `AgentControlMenu`),
    `Subagent` (→ `SubagentBubble`), `Questionnaire` (→ `ElicitationPrompt`), `Chat`
    (successor of `conversation.tsx`), `Terminal` and `Stack Trace`; none is installed.
    `chain-of-thought.tsx` and `inline-citation.tsx` are installed with zero product uses.
    `components.json` still points `@ai-elements` at the legacy ai-sdk.dev registry.
